# Landing page (/)

Scope: the marketing landing page and the site header, footer and demo dialog. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: a Build Games judge or a solo founder with a few minutes. Job: understand what Firsthand is and see the owner's side. Action: one, everywhere: sign in to the demo (email field, magic link into the shared demo workspace). Secondary exits live only in the footer and one line in the closing band.

Proof: the interview drawn in DOM (seeded demo conversation, labelled synthetic); measured embed numbers (1.1 KB, CLS 0, Lighthouse 100) from a production build on 3 Sept 2026; the deal as a term/definition spec sheet.

Constraints: no real customer testimonials exist for Firsthand itself; never invent counts, logos or press. No vendor names. Sibling discipline to SubbyFlow: one page, one action, no navigation, rigid section container (max-w-6xl px-5 sm:px-8 py-20 sm:py-28), one structural shape per section, purple only where you act.

Chosen direction: seven sections. Hero (centred, form, figure) → three outcomes on hairlines → ink band bento of five with drawn diagrams → two-column embed proof → spec sheet → FAQ (narrow, native details) → closing ink band. Memorable moment: the testimonial assembling from the customer's answers in the hero figure; the hand-drawn underline drawing in under "own words".

Unresolved: none for this surface. Dashboard navigation was collapsed to four items in the same pass (Testimonials, Collect, Show, Settings).
